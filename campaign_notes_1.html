Session I — The Seafarer's Roost, West Freeport

  • Party gathered at the Seafarer's Roost dockside tavern — inexplicably the only calm bar on the Freeport waterfront. No one knows why.
  • Met Commander Drebus Kell, mid-level Freeport Militia officer. Broad-shouldered, self-congratulatory, proud of his epaulettes. Both of them. Briefly.
  • Kell's briefing: necromantic activity to the south in troll/ogre territory — not crude shambling, but sophisticated. Some of the dead came back capable and magical. Militia suspects a hidden player.
  • Six weeks prior, Kell hired a Velanthra — a human wizard in her 50s, gray hair, full complement of eyelashes — to investigate Befallen (old elven city Takish-His, buried under the Serpent Spine in the Desert of Ro). She vanished. The militia cannot go themselves without making a political statement.
  • Contract terms: 100 gold on return, 25 hazard pay up front (5 each). The party is not eligible for standard hazard pay because, in Kell's estimation, they are the hazard.
  • Klaus attempted to steal a paper from Kell's pocket. Alarm spell triggered. Kell caught him — but offered the document anyway, since it contained additional guild job context he'd been holding back.
  • Dina attempted to negotiate a per-ear bounty for undead trophy collection. Kell declined. She pivoted to eyelashes. He declined that too. She offered eyelids. He left with half a beer still on the table.
  • A group of four Iskar (lizard folk diplomats — new to Antonica, clearly political figures) entered the tavern as the party was leaving. They ignored the party entirely. The party returned the favor.
  • Party departed south through the Commonlands by hired coach. Journey: roughly one day to the Tunnel.

"I have seen 5,000 springs. There is no novelty in the landscape for me anymore."

Interlude — The Coach South

Long travel. Party settled into character. Notable moments:

  • Patina revealed that 50 years ago, a child asking "what does metal dream about?" was the last time they felt real joy — and the question that prompted Bloom's creation.
  • Gwendolyn pressed Patina on Bloom's missing element. Patina explained: Bloom exists in a state of "mending" — returned to wholeness, but not alive. They seek something unknown to bring him to "cure wounds" — actual life. Not organic material. Origin unknown.
  • Gwendolyn's response: "You're looking for the slant of sunlight through leaves." Patina: "You're thinking about it too literally."
  • Klaus recounted a previous tunnel transit escorting Sir Feldergrass — a timid royal cousin, afraid of the dark, nose hair of legend. No fun. This trip is already better.
  • Dina began workshopping a peacock-call song with audience participation. Working title: "Panties for Death." Klaus is already dreading it.
  • Dina sent telepathic meows to Chris's absent character, directing them to meet the party at Befallen.

The Tunnel, Serpent Spine

The Tunnel — Location Notes

A marvel of engineering gifted by Qeynos to the Commonlands after a siege by dark elves and trolls cut off trade routes north and south. Bored through the Serpent Spine mountains. Roughly a quarter mile wide, 100 feet tall — far larger than any practical need dictated. Inhabited for centuries. Inside: cliff dwelling-style buildings carved directly into the rock walls with ladders connecting stacked levels, plus wood-frame stalls in the middle creating four rows of activity. Bustling like a busy street. No formal government — tribal block loyalty. Don't go into debt. Don't take credit. Don't talk to anyone named Charlie.

  • On arrival, Klaus located an old contact — a massive, perpetually cheerful man called "Dee" (full name unrecorded) running an aguas frescas stall with 3-foot serving cups. Old acquaintance from the Bazaar days. Gave Klaus a contact card stuck to the bottom of his drink.
  • Klaus bought five drinks for the party. Dee appears content but isolated — runs his stall alone, has not left in some time. Klaus promised to drag him out to the desert on the return trip.
  • Dina set her fiddle case down near an alley and opened with Baby Shark. Gwendolyn joined in dancing immediately, full enthusiasm, slightly off-key. Bloom was recruited as a dance partner. He moved his arms when Gwendolyn moved his arms.
  • Attracted approximately 10 children. All well-kept, range of ages. Dina shifted into a treasure-hunt song seeded with local landmarks and hiding spots, inviting the kids to fill in the geography. Coins in the case.
  • A child approached the fiddle case with deliberate, knowing eye contact and dropped a tiny folded note. It read: FOUND
  • Separately, Dee's contact card from the bottom of Klaus's drink also contained a message — likely YOU (context unclear at session end, but the two notes together appear to read "found you").
  • Klaus climbed onto his unseen servant's shoulders for a vantage point and scanned the crowd. Many eyes on the party — some curious, some hostile.
  • Someone moved up behind one of the party members and spoke aloud: "Found you."

World Notes — Antonica

Key Locations

  • Freeport — Major coastal city, east coast of Antonica. Metropolitan, militia-run, politically complex. Home base.
  • The Commonlands — Farmland south of Freeport under its jurisdiction. Temperate, rolling grassland. Choke point: dark elves to the north, trolls to the south.
  • The Tunnel — Qeynos-built passage through the Serpent Spine. Quarter mile wide, 100ft tall. Settled, lawless, tribally organized. Trade artery.
  • Desert of Ro — Formerly lush elven continent, scorched by Solusek Ro. Nomadic population, dock trade on the desert edge.
  • Befallen / Takish-Hiz — Ancient elven crown jewel, buried under the Serpent Spine when Solusek Ro raised the mountains in jealous wrath. Currently: presumed site of sophisticated necromantic activity. Destination.

Factions & Powers

  • Freeport Militia — Police and crooked politician in one. Hold the city by a thread, pull those threads into purse strings when able.
  • Wayfarers Guild — The party's employer. Gets done what others won't. Morally gray, compass pointed mostly north.
  • Iksar — Lizard folk, new to the continent. Politically active in Freeport. Native language: Cabalisian. Ambassadors seen in the Roost; rumors range from "misunderstood" to "run the government in disguise."
  • "The Organization" — A crew with fingers in all the tunnel's pies. Don't discuss. Don't owe them anything.
  • Solusek Ro — God of Magic, "The Burning Prince." His jealousy of elven magical prowess buried Takish-His and created the Desert of Ro. Ancient history with ongoing consequences.
