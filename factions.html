Factions of Norrath

The great organizations of Norrath — guilds, brotherhoods, orders, and cults — each with their own codes, ambitions, and reach. Understanding who these groups are is often the difference between an ally and an enemy.

WB
The Wayfarers Brotherhood
Est. ~11,800 · Norrath-wide · Freeport chapter active

A Norrath-wide organization of professional adventurers, investigators, and specialists who take contracts that official forces cannot, will not, or dare not handle. Politically neutral, racially inclusive, and answerable only to their own code and their clients.
